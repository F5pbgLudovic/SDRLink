The automatic notch detects steady whistles (carriers) in the WebSDR sound and attenuates them. A click on the button turns it on or off (green button when on, colours to choose below); right-click opens this window. Three types: original (4 to 8 narrow filters following the whistles; the level increases their number and width), adaptive (a filter removing everything that repeats, even a drifting whistle; the level sets its speed, a high level can affect the voice) and spectral (each frequency clearly above its neighbours for a while is brought down to their level; the cleanest, the level sets the threshold and the strength). Each type keeps its own level, 1 to 10. It is paused, without turning the button off, during a digital mode (decoder running or DIGI mode) and during FreeDV, if the choices above are ticked: it would damage the signals to decode. The SDRLINK cable and the decoders always receive the sound without notch.	Le notch automatique repère les sifflements fixes (porteuses) dans le son du WebSDR et les atténue. Trois types : d'origine (de 4 à 8 filtres étroits qui suivent les sifflements ; le niveau augmente leur nombre et leur largeur), adaptatif (un filtre qui retire tout ce qui se répète, même un sifflement qui glisse ; le niveau règle sa vitesse, un niveau élevé peut entamer la voix) et spectral (chaque fréquence qui dépasse nettement ses voisines pendant un moment est ramenée à leur niveau ; le plus propre, le niveau règle le seuil et la force).
