The automatic notch detects steady whistles (carriers) in the WebSDR sound and attenuates them. A click on the button turns it on or off (green button when on, colours to choose below); right-click opens this window. Three types: original (4 to 8 narrow filters following the whistles; the level increases their number and width), adaptive (a filter removing everything that repeats, even a drifting whistle; the level sets its speed, a high level can affect the voice) and spectral (each frequency clearly above its neighbours for a while is brought down to their level; the cleanest, the level sets the threshold and the strength). Each type keeps its own level, 1 to 10. It is paused, without turning the button off, during a digital mode (decoder running or DIGI mode) and during FreeDV, if the choices above are ticked: it would damage the signals to decode. The SDRLINK cable and the decoders always receive the sound without notch.	Automatski notch otkriva stalne zviždukove (nosioce) u zvuku s WebSDR-a i prigušuje ih. Klik na gumb uključuje ili isključuje notch (zeleni gumb kad je uključen, boje se biraju ispod); desni klik otvara ovaj prozor. Tri vrste: izvorni (4 do 8 uskih filtara koji prate zviždukove; razina povećava njihov broj i širinu), prilagodljivi (filtar koji uklanja sve što se ponavlja, čak i zvižduk koji luta; razina određuje njegovu brzinu, visoka razina može utjecati na glas) i spektralni (svaka frekvencija jasno iznad svojih susjeda neko vrijeme spušta se na njihovu razinu; najčišći, razina određuje prag i jačinu).
